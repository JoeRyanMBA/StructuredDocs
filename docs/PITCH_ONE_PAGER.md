# StructuredDocs — One-Page Overview

## What is StructuredDocs?

StructuredDocs is a modern platform that helps teams create, review, and publish professional documentation—faster and with higher quality. It replaces scattered files and email feedback with a single, organized workspace.

## The Problem We Solve

- Content is spread across tools with no clear ownership or history
- Review cycles are slow and unclear, delaying releases
- Formatting and publishing waste time and break consistency
- Leaders lack visibility into progress and bottlenecks

## What You Get

- Organized, structured content (Projects → Collections → Topics)
- Built-in review and approval workflows (including SMEs)
- Professional publishing (PDF, web, mobile knowledge base)
- Role-based access and stakeholder management
- Dashboards for status, throughput, and quality

## Business Outcomes

- Faster time-to-publish: shorten review cycles by 50–70%
- Higher content quality and consistency across teams
- Clear accountability and audit trails for compliance
- Reduced manual formatting and publishing effort (80–90%)
- Improved collaboration between authors and reviewers

## Why It Works

- Simple, intuitive workflows tailored to documentation teams
- Clear ownership and roles prevent handoff gaps
- Automated structure, formatting, and publishing
- Data-driven insights (status, approvals, cycle time)

## Where It Fits

- Product documentation, policies, training, and knowledge bases
- Regulated content with formal approvals
- Organizations scaling content output across multiple teams

## Getting Started

- Cloud-friendly deployment (Docker-ready)
- Rapid onboarding with templates and sample data
- Minimal training required; authors and reviewers get productive fast

## Summary

StructuredDocs gives you a single place to manage the full documentation lifecycle—from creation to approval to publication—so teams move faster, leaders gain visibility, and content stays professional and consistent.
