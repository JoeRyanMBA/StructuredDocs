# StructuredDocs — Executive Summary

> A one-stop platform for creating, reviewing, and publishing organizational knowledge — reliably, consistently, and at scale.

---

## The Problem We're Solving

Most organizations manage documentation the same way they always have: files scattered across shared drives, email threads carrying review comments, Word documents with "Final_v3_REAL_FINAL.docx" in the name, and no clear record of who approved what or when.

The result is predictable:

- **Outdated content** gets published because there's no structured update process
- **Institutional knowledge lives in people's heads** — or in files nobody can find
- **Reviews are ad hoc** — a reviewer's comment in an email is easy to miss, lose, or ignore
- **Publishing is a manual effort** — copying, reformatting, and distributing takes hours
- **Compliance is hard to prove** — "who signed off on this?" becomes an unanswerable question

StructuredDocs was built to fix all of that.

---

## What StructuredDocs Does

StructuredDocs is a **web-based content management platform** designed specifically for organizations that produce structured, high-stakes documentation — policies, procedures, knowledge bases, training materials, technical guides, and more.

It replaces the file-folder-email-export cycle with a single, structured workflow:

```
Create → Review → Approve → Publish
```

Every step is tracked, every decision is recorded, and every published document is consistent.

## Key Capabilities (Plain Language)

### 📁 Organized Content Structure
Content is organized in a three-level hierarchy: **Projects → Collections → Topics**. Think of a Project like a binder, Collections like sections in that binder, and Topics as individual pages. Everything has a logical home — nothing gets lost.

### ✍️ Rich Content Editing
Authors write using a full-featured web editor (similar to Word or Google Docs). Content can include text formatting, images, tables, lists, and hyperlinks. No separate software required.

### 📥 Document Import
Existing Word documents, PDFs, and Markdown files can be imported and automatically parsed into the structured hierarchy. Headings become Topics, images are preserved. This dramatically reduces the effort to migrate from legacy documentation systems.

### 📑 Reusable Content Snippets
Common content (disclaimers, standard procedures, legal language) is written once as a **Snippet** and inserted into multiple documents. When the source Snippet is updated, every document that uses it reflects the change — no more hunting down every place a policy appears.

### 🔁 Structured Review Workflow
Every piece of content can be routed through a formal review process:
1. An author submits a Topic for review
2. A reviewer receives a **secure, time-limited link** — no account required
3. The reviewer leaves inline comments directly on the content
4. The author responds to each comment (accept, reject, modify)
5. The review is formally approved or returned for revisions
6. A complete audit trail is automatically recorded

This is the difference between "I think someone looked at this" and "Review completed by Jane Smith on March 10, 2026, with 3 accepted changes."

### 📤 Professional Publishing
Approved content is assembled into a **Publication** and exported with one click as:
- A professionally formatted **PDF** (with cover page, table of contents, page numbers, and branding)
- A **web-based knowledge base** (accessible on any device, including mobile)
- A **self-contained HTML document** for offline distribution

No manual formatting. No copy-pasting into templates.

### ✅ Task Management
Tasks can be linked directly to Projects, Collections, or Topics. Assignees, due dates, priorities, and statuses are all tracked in one place — giving managers clear visibility into what's in progress and what's blocked.

### 📊 Real-Time Dashboards
The platform surfaces live metrics: how many topics are in draft vs. approved, how many reviews are pending, how many tasks are overdue. No status-update meetings needed to answer "where are we?"

## The Business Case

### Before StructuredDocs
- Documents live in email, SharePoint, and personal drives
- Reviews happen over email; comments are hard to track
- Publishing requires manual reformatting in Word or InDesign
- "What's the approved version?" requires calling someone
- Compliance audits require digging through email history

### After StructuredDocs
- All content is in one place, organized and searchable
- Reviews are formal, tracked, and auditable
- Publishing is a single button click
- Every document has a clear approval status and history
- Compliance evidence is automatically maintained

### Return on Investment

| Efficiency Gain | How |
|---|---|
| **Fewer revision cycles** | Structured inline feedback replaces scattered email threads |
| **Less time formatting** | Publishing templates handle layout automatically |
| **Faster onboarding** | New staff find approved content immediately; no tribal knowledge required |
| **Reduced compliance risk** | Every approval is logged with timestamp and reviewer identity |
| **Lower knowledge loss** | Content lives in the platform, not in individuals' inboxes |
